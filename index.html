Pick a team name, then submit an interval (a low and a high) for each question. The wider the interval, the higher the score; the further your interval is from the true answer, the higher your score. Lowest score wins.

Already signed in on this device? Entering the same team name puts you back on your team with your submissions intact.

How scoring works

  1. Each answer is an interval: a low and a high.
  2. You get 18 submissions total, spread across 15 questions however you like. Only your most recent interval per question counts.
  3. Every question costs 0.07 × (high ÷ low), so a narrow interval is cheap and a wide one is not.
  4. If your interval misses the answer, you also pay how far off you were, in doublings: an answer 4× outside your range adds 2, 8× adds 3.
  5. Skipping a question costs 10, almost always worse than a guess.
  6. Lowest total wins.
